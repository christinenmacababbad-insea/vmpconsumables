Privacy Policy

Shopee IMS Inventory Monitoring
Last updated: October 16, 2026

What this site is

Shopee IMS Inventory Monitoring is an internal inventory and consumables tracking website used by warehouse staff.

Information we collect

When you sign in, we collect only:

  • Your name and email address, either from your Google account (via Sign in with Google) or as typed into the sign-in form.
  • The warehouse you select.

We do not receive or store your Google password, contacts, files, or any other Google account data.

How we use it

Your name, email, and warehouse are used only to identify who entered or updated inventory records and to show your selected warehouse in the site. They are saved with the records you create.

Storage and sharing

Sign-in details are stored in your browser. Records you enter may be saved to the organization's shared Google Sheet used by this site. We do not sell your information or share it with advertisers or other third parties.

Retention and removal

Signing out removes your sign-in details from your browser. To request removal of your name or email from saved records, contact the site owner below.
